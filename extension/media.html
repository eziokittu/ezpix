Camera access help

In Chrome, open Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Camera. Choose the correct camera and allow EzPIX Insta if it was blocked.

On Windows, open Settings → Privacy & security → Camera. Enable Camera access and Let desktop apps access your camera. Close other apps using the camera, then choose Try camera again.
