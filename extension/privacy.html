EzPix SnapForge privacy

Updated 29 October 2026.

SnapForge processes images, captures, videos and palettes locally in your browser. It has no account, advertising, analytics, image upload or remote processing service. All executable code ships inside the extension.

What you choose to open

The toolbar captures only after your action. Page capture uses visible pixels. Camera and screen capture start through the browser permission chooser. Media is processed in local memory. Screen capture stops after a frame is taken; camera capture stops after taking a photo, pressing Stop, switching to Pixel studio or closing the studio.

Local retention

Manual artwork stays in the open Pixel studio tab. Project downloads preserve editable layers, frames and supported metadata. Imports are reviewed before replacing artwork; downloaded files are independent copies under your control.

Captured pixels, selection state and temporary transfers can be retained in extension IndexedDB for up to ten minutes. Expired records are removed when the extension wakes. Clear capture & pending handoff removes those records and resets conversion. It preserves manual artwork and recovery drafts.

Save recovery on this device is an explicit opt-in for each studio tab. Recovery retains up to four recent drafts, for seven days and at most 8 MiB total. Expired drafts are removed when the extension next wakes. Recovery includes artwork and references, but excludes original conversion images and Undo history. Review saved drafts offers explicit restore; nothing restores automatically. Delete saved drafts removes shared saved copies and pauses recovery in that tab. Another open tab with recovery enabled can save again. Browser cleanup or removing the extension can remove local data; keep project downloads as backups.

Appearance preferences remain in local browser storage. No artwork is stored in Chrome Sync.

Optional EzPix handoff

Open in EzPix website sends the prepared project through Chrome extension messaging to an authorised glitchbong.com editor tab, following your action and import confirmation. This is local browser messaging, not a server upload. Including the source image is optional and may retain pixels outside your crop. Manual-studio downloads and handoffs omit original conversion images. You can download images, animations, sprite sheets or editable project files without opening the website. The website's own privacy information and traffic analytics apply when you visit it.

Permissions

activeTab permits an explicitly requested capture of the active page. scripting installs the capture selector and checks page geometry. alarms expires temporary records. There is no blanket host, clipboard or browsing-history permission.
